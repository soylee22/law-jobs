How this search works

The min-jobs scanner runs about 18 targeted keyword searches (Mandarin legal, IP solicitor, China desk, trade mark, commercial litigation and more) across LinkedIn guest search, the CITMA jobs board, several company applicant-tracking feeds, and IP-specialist boards, then scores each role against the profile (IP, Mandarin, China, NQ band) and flags recruiter-fronted listings. It is the widest net and the apply-now pool. Fit score (0 to 100) = niche (IP, brand, China, cross-border) + level (NQ to mid) + a real legal-function title. The shortlist is ranked by it. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run. Use the filter buttons to narrow the full list.
