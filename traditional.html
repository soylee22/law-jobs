How this search works

The min-jobs scanner's curated specialist lane: targeted searches (Mandarin legal, IP solicitor, China desk, trade mark, commercial litigation) across LinkedIn guest search, the CITMA jobs board, several company applicant-tracking feeds, and IP-specialist boards, scored against the profile (IP, Mandarin, China, NQ band) with recruiter-fronted listings flagged. The broad general commercial, in-house and NQ counsel roles now have their own Open market tab; this tab is the IP, China and boutique pool. Fit score (0 to 100) = title lane (IP, brand, China) + level (NQ to mid) + employer/sector prior + optional JD-body signals on a selective subset (~50 roles/run). Three shortlist lanes: lane match, generic title at priority employers, on-ramp junior/NQ. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run; GONE marks high-fit roles that disappeared.
