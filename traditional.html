How this search works

The min-jobs scanner's curated specialist lane: targeted searches (Mandarin legal, IP solicitor, China desk, trade mark, commercial litigation) across LinkedIn guest search, the CITMA jobs board, several company applicant-tracking feeds, and IP-specialist boards, scored against the profile (IP, Mandarin, China, NQ band) with recruiter-fronted listings flagged. The broad general commercial, in-house and NQ counsel roles now have their own Open market tab; this tab is the IP, China and boutique pool. Fit score (0 to 100) = niche (IP, brand, China, cross-border) + level (NQ to mid) + a real legal-function title. The shortlist is ranked by it. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run.
