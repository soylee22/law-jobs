How this search works

We read vacancies straight from the own careers feeds of Chinese multinationals scaling in the UK and Europe (TikTok, ByteDance, Huawei, BYD, Shein, Tencent, JD, and more, about 27 companies), search each for legal terms, and keep UK/London legal roles. Fit score (0 to 100) = niche (IP, brand, China, cross-border) + level (NQ to mid) + a real legal-function title. The shortlist is ranked by it. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run.
