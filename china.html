How this search works

We read vacancies straight from the own careers feeds of Chinese multinationals scaling in the UK and Europe (TikTok, ByteDance, Huawei, BYD, Shein, Tencent, JD, and more, about 27 companies), search each for legal terms, and keep UK legal roles (UK-wide, not London-only). The point: these are employers where a PRC plus UK dual qualification is the asset, and a recognised-brand in-house role (Legal Counsel at TikTok) is mainstream work rather than a China-desk pigeonhole. Fit score (0 to 100) = title lane (IP, brand, China) + level (NQ to mid) + employer/sector prior, then a light Python JD scan (httpx + BeautifulSoup, no browser) of roles scoring ≥65 to pull PQE / IP-China signals and re-rank.
