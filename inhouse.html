How this search works

We read in-house legal vacancies straight from each company's own careers system (the same data feed its careers page calls), across the FTSE 100 plus a curated set of the biggest US firms (about 130 employers). For each we hit its applicant-tracking feed (Workday, Oracle HCM, SuccessFactors, SmartRecruiters, Greenhouse, Eightfold and a few bespoke APIs like Amazon, Google and Apple), search the feed for legal terms (legal, counsel, intellectual property, company secretary, compliance), and keep only roles whose title is a legal-function role located in the UK (any UK city or country-level UK token — not London-only). Location is shown on every role. This is not a job-board aggregator: every link opens on the employer's own careers domain. Fit score (0 to 100) = title lane (IP, brand, China) + level (NQ to mid) + employer/sector prior + optional JD-body signals on a selective subset (~50 roles/run). Three shortlist lanes: lane match, generic title at priority employers, on-ramp junior/NQ. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run; GONE marks high-fit roles that disappeared. Use the filter buttons to narrow the full list.
