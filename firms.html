How this search works

We read vacancies from UK IP, brand and China-desk law firms' own systems. Because every fee-earner role at a law firm is a legal job, the filter here is broader (associate, solicitor, trainee, paralegal, counsel) rather than only in-house legal keywords, and we keep UK roles (UK-wide, not London-only; location shown on each role). This is the on-ramp pool: a junior firm role in the right niche banks UK-counted PQE before a move in-house. We hit the legacy legal recruitment platforms directly (Candidats.io, CVMail, AllHires, Current-Vacancies, Eploy, Workable) as well as the mainstream ones, and best-effort scrape the server-rendered careers pages of the IP boutiques (HGF, EIP, Mathys & Squire). The short tail of firms with no machine-readable feed (JS-only pages or email/LinkedIn recruiting) is listed in an apply-direct panel below the live roles. Fit score (0 to 100) = title lane (IP, brand, China) + level (NQ to mid) + employer/sector prior, then a light Python JD scan (httpx + BeautifulSoup, no browser) of roles scoring ≥65 to pull PQE / IP-China signals and re-rank. Three shortlist lanes: lane match, generic title at priority employers, on-ramp junior/NQ. A red NEW badge means first seen today. Ex jobs tab = dropped off. Filters narrow the full list.
