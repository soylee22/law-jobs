How this search works

We read vacancies from UK IP, brand and China-desk law firms' own systems. Because every fee-earner role at a law firm is a legal job, the filter here is broader (associate, solicitor, trainee, paralegal, counsel) rather than only in-house legal keywords, and we keep UK/London roles. This is the on-ramp pool: a junior firm role in the right niche banks UK-counted PQE before a move in-house. We hit the legacy legal recruitment platforms directly (Candidats.io, CVMail, AllHires, Current-Vacancies, Eploy) as well as the mainstream ones; a short tail of firms that recruit only by email or a bespoke form are kept as direct links. Fit score (0 to 100) = niche (IP, brand, China, cross-border) + level (NQ to mid) + a real legal-function title. The shortlist is ranked by it. A red NEW tag marks roles unseen since the last run.
