How this search works

The job boards themselves, paginated as categories rather than as a handful of keyword searches. Hits are filtered to a legal-function title, recruiter-consultant ads are dropped, then de-duplicated by URL and by employer plus title plus town so the same role carried by three boards appears once. Roles at employers the In-house tab already covers are dropped. This is the widest route on the board.
