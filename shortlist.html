Legal roles · 2 August 2026

Twenty roles worth applying for

Ranked in the order to apply. Each entry states the seniority the advert asks for, what the work actually is, and the thing to watch before spending time on it.

The first five are this week's work.

  1. 1

    Ip Patents Disputes Associate

    Osborne Clarke

    Contentious IP at a major firm, and no seniority floor stated anywhere in the advert

    The densest IP role on the list that does not put a number in the candidate's way. Patent disputes at a firm with a serious technology practice, in London. Her four years were spent doing exactly this in China, and the ITC Section 337 investigation is the transferable proof: three Chinese clients, coordination across a US firm, a patent attorney on invalidity and CNIPA examiners running in parallel, won on non-infringement.

    Watch Patent litigation is technical. Her electrical automation degree helps; the absence of a UK patent track record does not.

    no PQE floor · posted 2026-07-19

  2. 2

    Ip Counsel Ip Platform Governance Emea

    Tiktok

    IP enforcement at platform scale, and the only qualification asked for is being an IP lawyer

    Managing high-impact IP disputes, building the IP governance policy for TikTok Shop and user-generated content, running internal IP training, protecting rights holders. It asks for “solid experience as an IP lawyer working in a top law firm or an in-house legal team” and stops there, with no numeric floor and no training-route requirement. Chinese parent, London seat.

    Watch Refreshed 31 July, so it has been open a while.

    no PQE floor · posted 2026-07-31

  3. 3

    Ip Counsel

    Oxford Pv

    Reporting to a Head of IP, on portfolio work and infringement

    Twenty-three separate references to intellectual property, the highest of anything in either sweep. Supporting commercialisation of the patent portfolio, capturing new IP with the R&D teams, instructing external advisers on filing and prosecution. The advert says experience of infringement, validity and freedom-to-operate would be valuable and prior IP litigation exposure an advantage, which describes the candidate's.

    Watch Yarnton, Oxfordshire, hybrid, and the advert carries fixed-term language. Establish the contract and the commute before anything else.

    2+ PQE · hybrid · posted 2026-07-29

  4. 4

    Legal Counsel Platform Liability

    Spotify

    Running takedown practice at a platform, with no PQE floor

    Supervising Spotify's legal takedown practices and policies, supporting the teams that write the content policies, and resolving the claims that come out of them. That is rights enforcement at volume under a different name, and it is the closest analogue on the list to the Bank of America work, where the candidate took down sites impersonating the bank.

    Watch Content and copyright rather than trade marks, and the advert says little about the level.

    no PQE floor · posted 2026-07-16

  5. 5

    Junior Litigation Lawyer (Qualified)

    Vanquis

    Junior, qualified, permanent, and one day a week in the office

    The title says junior and qualified and the advert means it: reporting to the Head of Litigation and supervising a team of Customer Litigation Specialists. London is one of four locations, and it invites part-time and flexible working. Of everything here it asks the least of the candidate's week.

    Watch High-volume consumer litigation, mainly return of goods. A long way from IP, and 98 people had applied inside three weeks.

    no PQE floor · usually one day a week in the office · posted 2026-07-11

Worth applying for, with a real stretch

Each asks for more than the profile has, or sits in the wrong city, or carries a condition to argue past.

  • Capeclarke, Intellectual Property Solicitor, tier-one US firm. 2+ PQE and £175,000 to £200,000, London. The money says the bar is very high, but the band is stated and the subject is exactly hers.
  • Bird & Bird, Associate, IP Life Sciences. No numeric floor, but it asks for a mid-level associate. Bird & Bird is the IP firm in Europe and appeared in the candidate's own Revolut preparation as external counsel, so the name recognition cuts both ways.
  • DAC Beachcroft, Associate, IP Disputes. 21 references to IP and a 3 PQE floor, but the seat is in Bristol.
  • Cursitor, Intellectual Property Associate. 3+ PQE, London, IP is the whole job. Two years above the candidate's.
  • Hays, Commercial Litigation NQ-2 PQE. The best-matched litigation band in the entire sweep, and it is in Manchester.
  • ECB Star Group, Junior Associate, Disputes, NQ to 2 PQE, elite US firm. The band is right and the advert says funds experience is advantageous rather than essential. Expect the pedigree screen.
  • William James Recruitment, Litigation Junior Associate. 1 PQE floor, hybrid, £100,000 quoted. Recruiter-fronted with no named client.
  • Illumina, Legal Counsel EMEALA. No floor stated, Cambridge, hybrid. The work is supply chain, procurement and manufacturing, which is not the candidate's ground.

Ruled out on pedigree, not on level

These adverts ask where the candidate trained, not only how long they have been qualified. No covering letter changes that.

  • Artificial Labs, Legal Counsel. NQ to 1 year PQE and IP in the job, but the advert asks three times for a City training contract, including “During your training contract you completed a client secondment or in-house seat” stated as fact. She qualified by SQE, in England, after four years in China, so the candidate has no training contract at all.
  • Formula 1, Legal Counsel, Dispute Resolution. 1 to 2 PQE and a disputes seat, but the requirement is flat: “Candidates will be able to demonstrate that they: Have trained and qualified as a lawyer at a leading law firm (e.g. City, US or West End law firm)”, and then that they worked as a junior lawyer in a leading firm after qualifying.

Advertised but no longer open

  • Alexander McQueen (Kering), Junior Legal Counsel. Kering's own careers site lists one Legal & Compliance vacancy in the whole of Europe, a Balenciaga IP internship in Paris. The LinkedIn posting still renders but has outlived the requisition, which was verified dead on 18 July.

Ruled out, and why

Floor above the candidate's

  • JD.com, IP & Brand Counsel: 7 to 10 years' PQE, and the second language it wants is European, not Mandarin
  • JD.com, Privacy Counsel and Marketplace Regulatory Counsel: 7 and 8 years respectively
  • Carpmaels & Ransford, Dispute Resolution Associate: 6 years
  • Quintessential Brands, Group Legal Counsel: 8 years
  • CITMA listing, Trade Mark Solicitor: minimum 4 years, c£120,000
  • Dawn Ellmore, Trade Mark Attorney / Solicitor: up to 5 years is a ceiling not a floor, but it is pitched at the attorney track and was posted on 1 May
  • Harbottle & Lewis, Commercial Litigation Associate: 4 to 5 PQE
  • Hydrogen Group, Commercial Litigation Associate: 5 PQE
  • KMA Legal, Senior Dispute Resolution Associate: 7 PQE, Guildford

Wrong jurisdiction or qualification

  • BP, Counsel, Digital, IP & Technology: Houston, Juris Doctor and a US state bar
  • Cisco, Corporate Counsel, AI Software and Platform: North Carolina
  • Visa, Senior Associate Counsel: San Francisco, JD and a State Bar
  • Salesforce, Counsel: Sydney and Melbourne, Australian practising certificate
  • Google, Associate Commercial Counsel, Research EMEA: JD or LLB plus three years' attorney-level experience

Right level, wrong place

  • G2 Legal, Commercial Litigation Solicitor: Manchester
  • Gleeson, Dispute Resolution Solicitor NQ-3 PQE: Derbyshire
  • Douglas Scott, Commercial Litigation Associate: Birmingham
  • Pinsent Masons, Commercial Litigation Associate: Leeds, 4 PQE
  • Aberdeen, Legal Counsel, Technology & Commercial Litigation: Edinburgh, 3 PQE
  • Motus Legal, Private Wealth Disputes Associate: Surrey, and private wealth is not the candidate's area
  • Howes Percival, Commercial, Data or IP Solicitor: Cambridge, 5+ PQE

Not the work

  • Amazon, Associate Corporate Counsel (Prime Video, Music, Leo): all 3+ years' post-qualification, and the candidate applied to Prime Video on 13 June with no reply
  • TikTok, Legal Counsel, eCommerce Product and Privacy Counsel: both want deep EU Digital Services Act or privacy specialism
  • LR Legal, Associate, Dispute Resolution: contentious trusts and probate
  • Court 1 Capital, International Arbitration Associate: sub-Saharan Africa focus
  • Coinbase, Counsel, Commercial: institutional crypto, remote UK, no floor but a specialist field
  • Salesforce, Corp Counsel: 12-month maternity cover, public sector
