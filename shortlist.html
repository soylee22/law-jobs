The full commercial contract suite including software licensing, SaaS, IT services, distribution, IP licensing and assignment, R&D collaboration with IP ownership negotiated as a term. Sectors: technology and SaaS, media and music, luxury and fashion, pharma, automotive, e-commerce, telecoms, manufacturing and R&D.

Constraints. London, Manchester or Bristol; fully remote acceptable.
