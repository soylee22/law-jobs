      Legal Counsel (IP), Product and Technology

      Sage

      Sits inside Sage's Product and Technology legal team, alongside the engineers and product managers building its accounting, payroll and HR software. The work is who owns what in a product, how the AI features can lawfully be built and sold, what the privacy rules require, and looking after the trade marks and brand as products go into new markets.

      The best fit anywhere on the board, and not a close call.

      • Essential list opens with a qualified solicitor and at least two years' post-qualification experience. The candidate is at roughly one and a half, the smallest gap of any IP role here.
      • It names experience supporting trademark and brand management activities as essential. That is four years of the candidate's working life, not a corner of it.
      • It also wants knowledge of IP, data privacy and AI. The Edinburgh IP LLM covers the first, and an electrical automation degree means the technology is not foreign.
      • Nothing anywhere about where the candidate trained or which university. Permanent, London, hybrid, and a real FTSE 100 legal team to learn inside.
      • Six months short on the stated number, so the letter has to convert Chinese IP practice into English PQE.
      • IP is one strand of the seat rather than the whole job.
