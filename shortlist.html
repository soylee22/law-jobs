Eighty adverts retrieved and read. Each role below is rated on the five things that decide whether an application is worth making, then ordered by how those ratings combine.

How each column is judged

Level
Does the candidate clear the seniority the advert asks for? The candidate is about a year and a half qualified in England and Wales.
Subject
How close the work is to intellectual property, brand enforcement or disputes.
Pedigree
Whether the advert gates on where the candidate trained. The candidate qualified through the SQE, not a City training contract.
Place
London or a reasonable commute, and how many days in the office.
Dual qual
Whether the candidate's PRC qualification and Mandarin are an asset here, or simply irrelevant.

Rated and rejected

Two of these were ranked highly on an earlier pass and are corrected here. Both were over-rated for the same reason: the advert stated no PQE number, which was read as a low bar when it meant the bar was expressed as demonstrated experience instead.

  • Osborne Clarke, IP (Patents) Disputes Associate. Ranked first before the advert was read properly, on the strength of its IP density and the absence of a PQE number. The absence was the trap. The requirement reads: “Knowledge and experience of UK patent litigation gained at a firm with a strong reputation in this area is essential”, with a biochemistry, biology, chemistry or immunology background highly beneficial, and the associate expected to lead more junior members of the team. Pharma and biotech patent disputes. The candidate has no UK patent litigation and the candidate's degree is electrical automation.
  • Voy, Legal Counsel, Litigation & Regulatory (Health-Tech). Also over-ranked at first. The advert wants years of contentious and regulatory experience plus hands-on life sciences regulation, pharmaceuticals or medical devices, ideally from a leading firm. The candidate has neither the sector nor the volume.
  • Artificial Labs, Legal Counsel. NQ to one year PQE with IP in the job, but it asks three times for a City training contract, including “During your training contract you completed a client secondment or in-house seat” stated as fact. The candidate qualified by SQE, in England, after four years in China.
  • Formula 1, Legal Counsel, Dispute Resolution. One to two PQE and a disputes seat, but candidates must “demonstrate that they have trained and qualified as a lawyer at a leading law firm (e.g. City, US or West End law firm)” and then worked as a junior lawyer in a leading firm.
  • Alexander McQueen (Kering), Junior Legal Counsel. The job no longer exists. Kering's own careers site lists one Legal and Compliance vacancy in the whole of Europe, a Balenciaga IP internship in Paris. The LinkedIn posting has outlived the requisition.
