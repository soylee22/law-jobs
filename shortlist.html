Thirteen roles, rated on what the adverts actually require

Eighty adverts retrieved in full and read to the requirements section. Each role below is rated on the five things that decide whether an application is worth making, then ordered by how those ratings combine.

How each column is judged

Level
Does the candidate clear the seniority the advert asks for? The candidate is about a year and a half qualified in England and Wales.
Subject
How close the work is to intellectual property, brand enforcement or disputes.
Pedigree
Whether the advert gates on where the candidate trained. The candidate qualified through the SQE, not a City training contract.
Place
London or a reasonable commute, and how many days in the office.
Dual qual
Whether the candidate's PRC qualification and Mandarin are an asset here, or simply irrelevant.

Rated and rejected

Four of these were ranked highly on an earlier pass and are corrected here. All four were over-rated for the same reason: the ranking was made from a summary of each advert rather than from its requirements section, so a qualification gate sitting near the foot of the page was never read.

  • Oxford PV, IP Counsel. Rated third on the previous pass and wrong. The essential skills open: “Fully qualified European Patent Attorney, preferably with at least 2–3 years’ post-qualification experience”, plus “a strong technical background in materials science, chemistry, or physics” and demonstrable patent drafting and prosecution. The candidate is a solicitor, not a patent attorney, and the candidate's first degree is electrical automation. The IP density that made it look strong was the reason it is closed.
  • Osborne Clarke, IP (Patents) Disputes Associate. Ranked first on the pass before that, also wrong. “Knowledge and experience of UK patent litigation gained at a firm with a strong reputation in this area is essential”, with biochemistry, biology, chemistry or immunology highly beneficial, and the associate expected to lead more junior members of the team.
  • Taylor Root, Associate, IP / IT. “Qualified lawyer with 2–4 years’ PQE at a leading firm (US, Magic Circle, Silver Circle, or specialist boutique)”, plus outstanding academic credentials. The pedigree requirement is written into the person specification, not implied.
  • EJ Legal, IP/IT &amp; Data Associate. “c.2–4 years’ PQE gained at a leading City, US or specialist technology practice”, with transactional IP experience. Same gate.
  • Capeclarke, Intellectual Property Solicitor. “2–4 years’ PQE within a leading City, Magic Circle, Silver Circle, US or specialist IP/Technology practice” and “excellent academics”. The £175,000 to £200,000 tells you what the firm expects to buy.
  • White &amp; Case, Associate, Commercial Litigation. “3–5 years’ experience within commercial litigation, gained wholly or in part at a large commercial law firm”, plus a strong academic record. Both the band and the background are out.
  • Taptap Send, Legal Counsel. Rated well on the previous pass on the strength of its cross-border work. The requirement is “legally qualified (any jurisdiction) with 4-7 years of experience”, so the level is wrong by some distance.
  • Voy, Legal Counsel, Litigation &amp; Regulatory. Years of contentious and regulatory experience plus hands-on life sciences regulation, pharmaceuticals or medical devices, ideally from a leading firm.
  • Artificial Labs, Legal Counsel. NQ to one year PQE with IP in the job, but it asks three times for a City training contract, including “During your training contract you completed a client secondment or in-house seat” stated as fact. The candidate qualified by SQE after four years in China.
  • Formula 1, Legal Counsel, Dispute Resolution. Candidates must “demonstrate that they have trained and qualified as a lawyer at a leading law firm (e.g. City, US or West End law firm)”.
  • TikTok, IP Counsel, IP Platform Governance, EMEA. Removed at Lee's instruction because an application may already have gone in. The applications ledger records a CV tailored for this exact role on 28 June and marks it not yet applied, so it is worth confirming: on the requirements it asks only for solid experience as an IP lawyer, with no years, no training route and no degree subject.
  • Alexander McQueen (Kering), Junior Legal Counsel. The job no longer exists. Kering's own careers site lists one Legal and Compliance vacancy in the whole of Europe, a Balenciaga IP internship in Paris.
