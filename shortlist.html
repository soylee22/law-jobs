Legal roles · 1 August 2026

Eighteen roles worth applying for

Ranked in the order to apply. Each entry states the seniority the advert asks for, what the work actually is, and the thing to watch before spending time on it.

The first five are this week's work.

  1. 1

    Legal Counsel

    Civica

    1 to 4 PQE, England and Wales, and it names trade marks and brand protection

    “Qualified Solicitor in England & Wales with 1-4 years' PQE”, and among the listed duties: “Advise on intellectual property matters, including licensing, trademarks and brand protection.” The business serves customers across the UK and APAC, so the international side is live rather than decorative. It also says the role suits someone who “wants exposure to senior stakeholders”, which is the gap the candidate's current job leaves.

    Watch Public-sector software, so the subject matter is unglamorous. Salary not published.

    1+ PQE · £25,000 · posted 2026-07-21

  2. 2

    Junior Litigation Lawyer (Qualified)

    Vanquis

    Junior, qualified, permanent, and one day a week in the office

    The title says junior and qualified, and the advert means it: reporting to the Head of Litigation and supervising a team of Customer Litigation Specialists. Hybrid at usually one day a week in the office, with London among four locations, and it invites part-time and flexible arrangements. Of everything on this list it asks the least of the candidate's diary.

    Watch High-volume consumer litigation, mainly return of goods. It is a long way from IP, and 98 people had applied within three weeks.

    no PQE floor · usually one day a week in the office · posted 2026-07-11

  3. 3

    Legal Counsel

    Ifrs Foundation

    No PQE floor, England and Wales, and the heaviest IP content of any role the candidate can actually reach

    Drafting commercial and IP contracts, and “protecting and maintaining intellectual property rights”, for an organisation whose main asset is a copyrighted standard. The team is a paralegal, a trainee, an Assistant Legal Counsel and a Legal Counsel under the Chief Legal Officer, so there is a defined junior rung. London.

    Watch The advert carries fixed-term language. Establish whether the post is permanent before spending time on it.

    no PQE floor · posted 2026-07-22

  4. 4

    Legal Counsel

    Huawei R&D UK

    Written for “around 2 years post SQE experience”, and the candidate has already applied

    Commercial, corporate, intellectual property and compliance for Huawei's UK research operation across London, Cambridge, Ipswich and Edinburgh. It is the only advert in the set that specifies the SQE route, which is how the candidate qualified, and the only one where the candidate's Mandarin is an operating asset rather than a nice line on the CV.

    Watch She applied on 30 June and has heard nothing. The listing is still live on Huawei's own site, so this is a chase, not a fresh application.

    no PQE floor · posted 2025-02-26

  5. 5

    Legal Counsel

    ICG (🇬🇧, Financial services)

    “English qualified lawyer (NQ – 4PQE)”

    NQ is the bottom of the stated range rather than a stretch, and the advert says it is open to candidates qualified elsewhere too, which quietly helps a dual-qualified applicant. Intercontinental Exchange runs exchanges and clearing houses, so it is a serious in-house function in London.

    Watch Financial markets regulation, not IP. This one is about getting into a good in-house team at the right level.

    1+ PQE · posted 2026-05-13

  6. 6

    Associate Counsel

    Paramount

    1 to 3 PQE, and the seat is Consumer Products, which is licensing

    Consumer Products and Experiences at a global studio is trade mark licensing, brand approvals and merchandising agreements. That is soft IP under another name, and it is the closest thing on the list to what the candidate did for brand owners in China. Posted 31 July, so it is fresh.

    Watch Camden Town and on-site.

    1+ PQE · on-site · posted 2026-07-31

The process is transparent, with a stage against the General Counsel and Senior Legal Counsel on commercial experience and contract negotiation, which is a fair test of what the candidate has.

    Watch Gaming and player experience, so consider whether the sector suits the candidate's.

    1+ PQE · posted 2026-07-24

  14. 14

    Watch It is pitched at the attorney track as well as solicitors, so the candidate would be competing against qualified trade mark attorneys. Posted 1 May, so check it is still open.

    5+ PQE · posted 2026-05-01

Advertised but no longer open

  • Alexander McQueen (Kering), Junior Legal Counsel. The LinkedIn posting still renders, three weeks old with over 200 applicants, but Kering's own careers site lists exactly one Legal & Compliance vacancy in Europe and it is a Balenciaga intellectual property internship in Paris starting January 2027. Requisition R167842 was already verified dead on 18 July. The role has now been resurrected twice from a LinkedIn listing that outlived it. For Kering, kering.com is the only source worth believing.

Ruled out on pedigree, not on level

These adverts ask where the candidate trained, not only how long they have been qualified. the candidate qualified through the SQE, in England, after four years practising in China. She has no training contract, and no covering letter changes that.

  • Artificial Labs, Legal Counsel. Ranked first on the PQE band and the IP content before this was read properly. The advert opens “If you trained at a top London firm and did an in-house seat”, then asks for someone “ideally trained at a leading London law firm (Magic Circle, Silver Circle, leading City or top US firm)”, and then states as fact: “During your training contract you completed a client secondment or in-house seat.” the candidate qualified through the SQE rather than a training contract, and not in London. Two of those three the candidate cannot answer at all.
  • Formula 1, Legal Counsel, Dispute Resolution. 1 to 2 PQE and a disputes seat, which fits. But the requirement is not hedged: “Candidates will be able to demonstrate that they: Have trained and qualified as a lawyer at a leading law firm (e.g. City, US or West End law firm)”, and then “following qualification, have worked as a junior lawyer in a leading law firm or as part of a reputable in-house team”. That is a screen the candidate fails twice.
  • SSQ, Legal Counsel. Milder, and probably survivable: “Strong academics and training from a recognised law firm.” Recognised is a lower bar than leading, and it does not name London.

Worth a look, with a real stretch

Each asks for more than the candidate has or carries a condition the candidate does not meet. Listed because the subject matter is close enough to justify applying anyway.

  • Major, Lindsey & Africa, Legal Counsel, Compliance (TMT & E-Commerce). 4 to 8 years, so a real stretch, but it is the only advert of 87 that makes Chinese a core requirement: “English and Chinese”, “Chinese communication (core requirement)”. It accepts bar admission in England and Wales, the US “or other common law/civil law jurisdiction”, which is written for exactly the candidate's profile. Six-month interim.
  • TradingView, Legal Counsel. 3 to 5 years PQE, London hybrid, and the advert wants a “subject matter expert in market data, IP, and disputes”. IP and disputes in the same sentence is rare.
  • Ultralytics, Legal Counsel. No PQE floor, England and Wales, London. Owns the trade mark, copyright and trade secret portfolio and instructs counsel in the UK, Spain and China. But it says plainly: “We need someone who's done this kind of work before, not someone learning open-source licensing on the job.”
  • Gotion High Tech, Legal Counsel. 3 to 7 PQE at a Chinese battery manufacturer in London, with Mandarin named as a plus.
  • Visa, Sr. Associate Counsel (London). No PQE floor stated, London hybrid, and it asks for a lawyer “qualified in England & Wales” while adding that it will consider other jurisdictions. The Sr. in the title is the risk.
  • Amazon, Associate Corporate Counsel (Prime Video, Music, Leo). All ask for 3+ years' post-qualification experience. She applied to Prime Video on 13 June and has not heard, so a second Amazon application adds little.
  • Major, Lindsey & Africa, Interim Junior Regulatory Legal Counsel. “Ideally 2–6 PQE, although all relevant profiles” considered. £100,000 to £120,000, London hybrid, interim.
  • Bending Spoons, Graduate Legal Counsel. Aimed at limited experience and pays £85,797 in London on that basis. One of the core teams the candidate could support is intellectual property. Fully remote.

  • JD.com, IP & Brand Counsel: 7 to 10 years' PQE, and the second language it wants is German, French, Dutch, Spanish or Portuguese
  • Kingsley Green, Legal Counsel, Intellectual Property (global consumer brand): 5 to 7 years' PQE. The most painful miss on the list: a pure IP seat at a household-name brand
  • Happyrobot, Legal Counsel: 6+ years of relevant legal experience

  • CITMA listing, Trade Mark Solicitor: minimum 4 years' PQE

  • Anthropic, Commercial Counsel, EMEA: 4+ years, £125,000 to £165,000

  • Manolo Blahnik, Legal Counsel (maternity cover): 4 to 8 years PQE, and the focus is real estate

  • Google, Associate Commercial Counsel, Research EMEA: JD or LLB plus three years' attorney-level experience, and the posting sits in the US
  • Salesforce, Counsel: Sydney and Melbourne, with an Australian practising certificate

Wrong practice area

  • HSPG, Junior Legal Counsel: the level is right at NQ to 2 PQE, but it is a real estate portfolio role and residential transactional experience is called essential
  • Heriot Brown, Legal Counsel: corporate, M&A, investment funds
  • Novo Resourcing, In House Legal Counsel, Corporate: private equity and venture capital deal work
  • Brightsmith, Legal Counsel: projects, construction and infrastructure, four to six years
  • Simmons Hanbury, Legal Counsel, Global Retailer: real estate at its core
  • Montagu Evans, Commercial Counsel: a property firm
  • Egis, Legal Counsel: infrastructure, 3 to 4 years PQE
  • Roche, Legal Counsel: life sciences experience required, Welwyn, fixed term

Right level, wrong place

  • Debenhams Group, Legal Counsel: explicitly says 2+ years PQE “is not essential”, and the work spans IP, advertising and consumer law, but it is Manchester and on-site
  • Rolls-Royce SMR, Legal Counsel: Derby, Manchester or Warrington. £66,240 to £86,940
  • Jameson Legal, Commercial Litigation Solicitor 1-3 PQE: the band is right but the role is in Surrey
