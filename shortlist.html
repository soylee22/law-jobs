150 roles scored, best to worst

150 adverts pulled from the scanner on prima facie sense, then each one retrieved in full and read to its requirements section. Every role is scored out of 100. Where the requirements contradicted the score, the judgement is written underneath.

How the score is built

Level ×5Does the candidate clear the seniority the advert asks for. The candidate is about a year and a half qualified in England and Wales. A qualification the candidate cannot hold, such as a patent attorney or a foreign bar, scores zero.
Subject ×4How close the work is to intellectual property, brand enforcement and disputes.
Pedigree ×4Five means the advert does not care where the candidate trained. Zero means it names Magic Circle, Silver Circle or an elite firm.
Place ×3From Wembley. London and remote score five, the outer commute three, Manchester or Oxford one.
Dual qual ×2Whether the candidate's PRC qualification and Mandarin are wanted, or simply irrelevant.
Field ×2How many people have already applied.
