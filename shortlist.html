Legal roles · 2 August 2026

Sixteen roles, ranked by how well they actually fit

Drawn from 150 adverts pulled off the scanner and read in full. Ranked on judgement rather than arithmetic: what the employer is really asking for, set against what the candidate has done. The case for each is above the line, the case against below it.

  1. 1

    Legal Counsel (IP), Product and Technology

    Sage

    The best fit on the board, and not a close call.

    • The essential list asks for a qualified solicitor with at least two years' PQE. The candidate is at about one and a half, so this is the smallest gap of any IP role here.
    • It names experience supporting trademark and brand management activities as essential. That is the centre of four years of the candidate's working life, not a corner of it.
    • IP sits with data privacy and AI, and the candidate has the Edinburgh IP LLM plus an engineering first degree, so the technology side is not foreign.
    • Nothing anywhere in the advert about where the candidate trained or what university the candidate went to.
    • FTSE 100, permanent, London, hybrid. A proper legal function with people to learn from, which the candidate's current job cannot give the candidate's.
    • Six months short on paper, so the covering letter has to convert four years of Chinese IP work into the two years they asked for.
    • IP is one of several things the seat covers rather than the whole job.
  2. 2

    Associate Counsel

    Paramount

    Reads as though someone wrote it after looking at the candidate's CV.

    • One to three PQE, so the candidate is inside the band rather than arguing the candidate's way in.
    • It expressly welcomes applicants from a private practice background who have managed their own caseload and worked directly with clients. The candidate has run the candidate's own matters and dealt with clients and opponents direct since qualifying.
    • It wants a strong understanding of IP law, ideally across multiple jurisdictions, and calls experience across different countries a plus. Almost nothing else on the list rewards the second qualification instead of tolerating it.
    • The work is rights checks, clearances, IP ownership and the licensing agreements that follow. That is brand protection under a studio's name for it.
    • Camden Town, which from Wembley is about twenty minutes.
    • On-site rather than hybrid.
    • A hundred applicants already, and a studio name attracts people.
  3. 3

    Legal Counsel

    Nominet

    Undersold by its title. This is brand enforcement work at the .uk registry.

    • Domain suspension queries, dispute resolution escalations, the DRS policy and the criminal practices policy. Cybersquatting and impersonation are exactly what the candidate handled for Bank of America when the candidate took down sites using the bank's name.
    • Settlement negotiations and instructing external counsel, so the candidate keeps running matters rather than only drafting.
    • No numeric PQE floor at all. It asks for a qualified solicitor with proven post-qualification experience and leaves it there.
    • Permanent, London or Oxford, and hybrid at a minimum of one day a week in the office.
    • Half the role is technology contracts, government frameworks and cyber security regulation, which is new ground.
    • It asks for experience of government contracts, which the candidate has none of.
  4. 4

    Legal Counsel

    Oner Active

    A junior in-house seat where the candidate would own the brand's IP outright.

    • Explicitly a junior/mid level lawyer, so the level is right.
    • Overseeing the IP portfolio is one of the listed responsibilities. In-house roles that hand the portfolio to a junior lawyer are rare.
    • Talent agreements and third-party contracts across tech, events and facilities, so genuine breadth in a consumer brand.
    • Company secretarial and governance work would round out a CV that is currently narrow.
    • A young apparel business, so there is no legal team to learn from.
    • Salary is not published and a brand this size will not pay what the banks pay.
  5. 5

    Legal Counsel

    Huawei R&D UK

    Still the best-calibrated advert of any, and the candidate has already applied.

    • Around two years post SQE, and it is the only advert in either sweep that names the SQE route the candidate qualified by.
    • IP is in the job description, not adjacent to it: research and development contracts, IP provisions in commercial agreements, licensing.
    • Mandarin is an operating asset here rather than a line on a CV.
    • Real breadth: procurement, research partnerships with universities, export control, anti-bribery.
    • Office-based with Cambridge preferred, or frequent travel to Cambridge. From Wembley that is a serious commitment.
    • It asks for qualifications from a top-tier university.
    • The candidate applied on 30 June and has heard nothing, so this is a chase rather than a fresh application.
  6. 6

    Legal Counsel

    Shaneel Enterprises

    The rare advert that treats a foreign qualification as a qualification.

    • It asks for a UK or foreign-qualified lawyer, and then says it will consider candidates outside the 1 to 5 PQE range with suitable skills. Two doors held open in one sentence.
    • Consumer products, so trade marks and brand come with the territory even though the advert leads on commercial contracts.
    • Contract law and commercial contracts are what the candidate does day to day now.
    • It names French, Spanish or German as the advantageous languages, not Mandarin.
    • A smaller business, so less structure and less supervision.
  7. 7

    Associate Legal Counsel

    Legend

    Right level, fair process, ordinary work.

    • One to three PQE, England and Wales, London.
    • The published process puts a sixty-minute round with the General Counsel on commercial experience and contract negotiation. That tests what the candidate can do, not where the candidate trained.
    • Four stages set out in advance, which suits someone who prepares.
    • Consumer gaming, and the IP content is incidental.
    • Seventy-seven applicants.
  8. 8

    Legal Counsel

    Sportfive

    The cleanest level match in the whole set.

    • UK qualified solicitor (NQ to 2 years' PQE), stated plainly. The candidate is squarely inside it.
    • Sports and media rights work is licensing and image rights, which is closer to the candidate's ground than the title suggests.
    • The advert asks for interest and attitude rather than pedigree.
    • No IP in the job description as such.
    • A genuine interest in sport is called for, and the candidate would have to mean it.
  9. 9

    Legal Counsel

    ICG

    Strong on level and process, weak on subject.

    • Private practice trained with one to four years' PQE, which describes the candidate's exactly.
    • It says in terms that it will consider all applications that meet the requirements regardless of the number of years. Very few adverts say that and mean it.
    • London with flexible working, and a structured group legal team.
    • Alternative asset management has nothing to do with IP, brands or disputes.
    • It asks for experience of corporate, commercial or funds law, and funds would be new.
  10. 10

    Associate Counsel

    Cummins Europe

    The safe institutional option.

    • No PQE floor at all. It asks for a corporate and commercial background, which the candidate has.
    • Project agreement and M&A experience is preferred but not essential, so the gaps are acknowledged in advance.
    • A large European legal function, so supervision, training and a name on the CV.
    • Some claims and litigation management alongside the global litigation team.
    • Industrial engineering, and the closest it gets to IP is product compliance.
    • Three days a week in the office, and 144 applicants.
  11. 11

    Legal Counsel

    LHH (client undisclosed)

    Wide open on level, thin on everything else.

    • One to five years' PQE in England and Wales, and it will also consider people who have done the work in-house without the title.
    • Commercial contract drafting and negotiation, which is the strongest part of the candidate's current role.
    • Recruiter-fronted with no named client, so there is nothing to research and nothing to tailor to.
    • No IP, no disputes, no cross-border angle.
  12. 12

    NQ Legal Counsel

    Robert Walters (client undisclosed)

    Best money of anything the candidate can actually get.

    • Written for a newly qualified lawyer and it says so.
    • £80,000 to £95,000 published, well above the candidate's £45,000 to £65,000 band.
    • Insurance experience is preferable but expressly not required.
    • Broad exposure: underwriting, claims, contracts, corporate, compliance and complaints.
    • Lloyd's market insurance is a specialised world and nothing in the candidate's CV points at it.
    • A small team, so limited supervision, and the salary implies a high bar on quality.
  13. 13

    Legal Counsel (In-House)

    Quantum Motion

    IP and licensing work at a London deep-tech company, with no number attached.

    • No numeric PQE floor. It asks for post-qualification experience in commercial or IP law without saying how much.
    • Licence agreements and technology transfer arrangements are named responsibilities, which is IP commercialisation.
    • London, and quantum computing means the IP is the company's main asset.
    • It wants that experience gained in a reputable law firm or an R&D-intensive in-house team, and asks for a strong academic background.
    • Deep-tech, so the subject matter is demanding.
  14. 14

    Commercial Litigation & International Arbitration Associate

    Sonder Consultants

    Worth an application purely for one sentence.

    • It welcomes applications from England and Wales qualified solicitors and overseas-qualified lawyers with relevant disputes experience. Of everything read across three days this is the only advert where the candidate's PRC qualification counts toward the requirement rather than needing to be explained away.
    • Cross-border disputes across multiple jurisdictions and legal systems, which is the candidate's actual background.
    • Two to four PQE gained at a recognised disputes practice, so the candidate is short on both counts.
    • Over 200 people have applied.
    • Client undisclosed, described only as a leading international law firm.
  15. 15

    Legal Counsel (12-month contract)

    Wood

    Right level, wrong shape.

    • NQ to 3-year post qualification experience, in-house or private practice, so no argument needed.
    • Commercial contracting fundamentals plus disputes awareness, both of which the candidate has.
    • Twelve-month contract, and the candidate is leaving a job for stability as much as for the work.
    • Energy services, with no IP content at all.
  16. 16

    Junior Litigation Lawyer (Qualified)

    Vanquis Banking Group

    The most open door on the list, into the least interesting room.

    • It asks for a qualified lawyer with litigation experience or someone with a clear interest in building expertise in this area. Motor finance experience is beneficial but not essential.
    • Supervisory from day one, running a team of Customer Litigation Specialists.
    • Usually one day a week in the office, with part-time and flexible working invited. Nothing else here asks so little of the candidate's week.
    • High-volume return-of-goods consumer claims. Repetitive by design, and it builds nothing the candidate wants to be known for.
    • It would move the candidate's further from IP, not closer.
    • 98 applicants inside three weeks.

Read and rejected

Each of these looked plausible from the title or the summary. The quoted requirement is why it is not.

  • Osborne Clarke, IP (Patents) Disputes Associate. “Knowledge and experience of UK patent litigation gained at a firm with a strong reputation in this area is essential”, with biochemistry or biology highly beneficial, and the associate expected to lead junior members of the team.
  • Oxford PV, IP Counsel. Essential skills open with “Fully qualified European Patent Attorney” plus a strong technical background in materials science, chemistry or physics. The candidate is a solicitor and the candidate's first degree is electrical automation.
  • TradingView, Legal Counsel. Three to five years' PQE, and the seat is market data and financial regulation at a trading platform. The IP references that made it look strong are about the company's own product, not the job.
  • Oxford Quantum Circuits, Legal Counsel. No PQE floor and real IP commercialisation content, but it wants a track record in deep-tech, engineering or research environments and the office is in Reading.
  • Civica, Legal Counsel. 1-4 PQE with IP and licensing exposure highly desirable, then: “Training gained within a leading City, national or international law firm.”
  • Elekta, Legal Counsel, Region Europe. Asks for a graduate of an English law school with strong academic credentials. Her LLM is Edinburgh and the candidate's first degree is Chinese.
  • White & Case, Associate, Commercial Litigation. 3-5 years gained wholly or in part at a large commercial law firm.
  • EJ Legal, IP/IT & Data Associate. c.2-4 years' PQE gained at a leading City, US or specialist technology practice.
  • Taylor Root, Associate, IP / IT. 2-4 years' PQE at a leading firm, named as US, Magic Circle, Silver Circle or a specialist boutique.
  • Capeclarke, Intellectual Property Solicitor. 2-4 PQE within a leading City, Magic Circle, Silver Circle, US or specialist IP practice, plus excellent academics.
  • Mishcon de Reya, Associate, Tech Disputes. 3-5 PQE gained in commercial, IT or tech disputes in private practice, and it wants a subject matter specialist.
  • Bird & Bird, Associate, IP Life Sciences. A mid-level associate at the leading IP firm in Europe, so several years above the candidate's.
  • Taptap Send, Legal Counsel. Legally qualified in any jurisdiction with 4-7 years of experience.
  • Ultralytics, Legal Counsel. Hands-on experience negotiating or enforcing AGPL and GPL open-source licences, converting unauthorised commercial use into paying customers, and operating as the sole in-house lawyer.
  • Voy, Legal Counsel, Litigation & Regulatory. Years of contentious and regulatory experience plus hands-on life sciences regulation, pharmaceuticals or medical devices.
  • Artificial Labs, Legal Counsel. NQ to one year PQE with IP in the job, but it asks three times for a City training contract, one of them stated as a fact about the candidate.
  • Formula 1, Legal Counsel, Dispute Resolution. Candidates must demonstrate that they trained and qualified at a leading law firm, given as City, US or West End.
  • Alexander McQueen, Junior Legal Counsel. The requisition no longer exists. Kering's own careers site carries one Legal and Compliance vacancy in Europe, a Balenciaga internship in Paris.
  • TikTok, IP Counsel, IP Platform Governance. Removed at the client's request pending confirmation that an application already went in. On the requirements it asks only for solid experience as an IP lawyer, with no years and no training route.
Every judgement is made from the requirements section of the employer's own advert.
