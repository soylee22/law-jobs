How this search works

The open-market net: about a dozen broad LinkedIn guest searches (legal counsel, in-house counsel, commercial counsel, corporate counsel, newly qualified solicitor, commercial contracts, data protection, technology lawyer and more) across London and the wider UK, including remote and national postings. This is the long tail of employers the In-house tab cannot see (mid-size companies, scale-ups, recruiters posting direct), kept to Min's lanes (commercial, corporate, IP and brands, disputes, data and tech) and hard-filtered out of the wrong practice areas (property, immigration, family, personal injury, crime, private client). The widest, freshest apply-now pool. Fit score (0 to 100) = title lane (IP, brand, China) + level (NQ to mid) + employer/sector prior, then a light Python JD scan (httpx + BeautifulSoup, no browser) of roles scoring ≥65 to pull PQE / IP-China signals and re-rank. Three shortlist lanes: lane match, generic title at priority employers, on-ramp junior/NQ. A red NEW badge means first seen today. Ex jobs tab = dropped off. Filters narrow the full list.
